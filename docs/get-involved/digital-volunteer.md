# Digital Detectives 

Can you help discover and share the stories from Toowong Cemetery? 

We have a large collection of guided tour brochures that have been scanned and need to be transcribed so they can be published on this site. 

No special software is needed and we've got instructions and templates to get you started. 


![Markdown plain text is converted into webpages](../assets/markdown-example.jpg){ width="90%" }


Go further with your detective work and investigate the people mentioned in the stories. 


## Can you help?

Do as little or a much as you like, when and where it suits you.
