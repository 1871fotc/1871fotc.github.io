# Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card - coming soon

Get your free Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card to add to your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. 

More than just a visitor guide, the Explorer Card helps you explore the cemetery, stay informed, and remain connected with the Friends of Toowong Cemetery.

### Explore More During Your Visit

The Explorer Card puts helpful information at your fingertips, including quick access to:

- Find a Grave - complete with directions to the grave you're looking for
- Friends of Toowong Cemetery stories - ...
- Self-guided walks - ...
- Research resources and points of interest

Whether you're searching for a family grave or discovering Queensland's history, the Explorer Card makes it easier to get the most from every visit.

### Discover Stories Around You 

As you explore the cemetery, your Explorer Card can notify you when you're near notable graves, monuments and places of historical significance.
You may discover the resting places of pioneering doctors, decorated soldiers, civic leaders, artists, sporting identities and other remarkable Queenslanders whose stories helped shape our state.
It's like having a heritage guide in your pocket.

### Stay Informed

When you download your Explorer Card, you're subscribed to our email newsletter so you're always  
up to date with our guided tours, and volunteer events.

### A Card That Grows With You

The Explorer Card reflects your journey with the Friends of Toowong Cemetery.

- Begin with a Visitor Explorer Card.
- Attend your first volunteer event and your card becomes a Heritage Hero Card, recognising your volunteering contribution and connecting you to our rewards programme.
- Become a member and your card becomes a Friends of Toowong Cemetery Membership Card, recognising your ongoing contribution, recording your years of membership and helping you stay on top of membership renewals.

**Download your free Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card - Start exploring, stay connected, and become part of the story.**
