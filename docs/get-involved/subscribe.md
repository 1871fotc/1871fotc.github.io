# Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card - coming soon

The Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card is your digital companion for exploring the cemetery and staying connected with the Friends of Toowong Cemetery.

<!-- image -->

Add it to your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet to:

- Find graves and navigate directly to them
- Discover stories and points of interest nearby
- Receive updates about guided tours and volunteer events
- Stay connected with Friends of Toowong Cemetery activities

Your card evolves as your involvement grows, from an Toowong Cemetery explorer, to Heritage Hero volunteer, to Friends of Toowong Cemetery Member.
