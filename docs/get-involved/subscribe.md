# Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card 

### Coming soon

The **Toowong Cemetery Explorer Card** is your digital companion for exploring the cemetery and staying connected with the Friends of Toowong Cemetery.

<!-- image -->

Add it to your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet to:

- :fontawesome-solid-search-location: Find graves and get directions
- :fontawesome-solid-location-dot: Discover stories and interpretive displays nearby
- :fontawesome-solid-bell: Receive updates about guided tours and volunteer events
- :fontawesome-solid-envelope: Stay connected with Friends of Toowong Cemetery news

Your card evolves as your involvement grows, from a Toowong Cemetery Explorer, to a Heritage Hero Volunteer, to a Friends of Toowong Cemetery Member.
