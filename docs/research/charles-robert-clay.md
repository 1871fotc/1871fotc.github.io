- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 31 July 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as C. R. Clay
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014
