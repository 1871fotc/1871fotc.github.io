# Stanhope Holford Wettenhall

#foundHeadstones  


**22 June 1872 — 11 April 1874**

## Stanhope Holford Wettenhall <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="wettenhall-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" /> 

<small>*A fragment of Stanhope Holford Wettenhall's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 11 April 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as S. H. Wettenhall
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.

### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of  <br>
> Isla Frances    <br>
> daughter of  <br>
> Allan Ravenscroft   <br>
> and <br>
> Sarah Anne Wettenhall <br>
> born 31^st^ March 1864 <br>
> died 28^th^ April 1864 <br>
> Also <br>
> Allan Ravenscroft <br>
> son of the above <br>
> born 16^th^ November 1867 <br>
> died 30^th^ June 1871 <br>
> Also <br>
> Stanhope Holford <br>
> son of the above <br>
> born 22^nd^ June 1872 <br>
> died 11^th^ April 1874 <br>
