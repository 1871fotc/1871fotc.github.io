# John Mace

**Died 11 September 1869**

## John Mace <small>([Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of John Mace's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/john-mace-headstone-2015.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of John Mace's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 11 September 1869
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as John Mace
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015

### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of <br>
> John Mace <br>
> who was <br>
> accidentally drowned <br>
> in the River Brisbane <br>
> on the 11 of September 1869 <br>
> aged 23 years <br>
> Erected <br>
> as a tribute of respect <br>
> by a few of his <br>
> surviving friends <br>
