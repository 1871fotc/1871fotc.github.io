# Alexander Scott

**Died 16 February 1850**

## Alexander Scott <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Alexander Scott's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/alexander-scott-headstone-2021.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Alexander Scott's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2021.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 16 February 1850
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Alex. Scott
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2021

### Inscription

> …otland <br>
> … this life  <br>
> …arong<br>
> …   <br>
