### Headstone

<small>*Fragments of John Muir's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs in 2021 and 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 21 August 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. Muir
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. 22 May 2021 and ca. May 2023
