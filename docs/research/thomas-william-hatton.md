# Thomas William Hatton

**ca. 1853 — 1 July 1874**

## Thomas William Hatton <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of Thomas William Hatton's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/thomas-william-hatton-headstone-2021-1.jpg){ width="50%" class="full-width" loading=lazy } 
![Fragment of Thomas William Hatton's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/thomas-william-hatton-headstone-2021-2.jpg){ width="28%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Thomas William Hatton's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2021.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 1 July 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Thomas William Hatton
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  22 May 2021

### Inscription

> In memo… <br>
> of <br>
