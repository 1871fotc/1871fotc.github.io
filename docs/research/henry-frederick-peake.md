# Henry Frederick Peake

**Died 2 May 1871**

## Henry Frederick Peake <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499)))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Henry Frederick Peake's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/amelia-isabel-peake-headstone-2012.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Henry Frederick Peake's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 2 May 1871
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. F. Peake
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory of <br>
> Amelia Isabel Peake <br>
> Died 22^nd^ April 1873 <br>
> Aged 24 years  <br>
> also her sons <br>
> Henry Frederick <br>
