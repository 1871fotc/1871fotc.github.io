# John Christian Edwards

#foundHeadstones

**25 December 1868 — 21 July 1873** 

## John Christian Edwards <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="john-christian-edwards-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*John Christian Edwards' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2016.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 21 July 1873  
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Records as Edwards
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2016

### Inscription

> To the memory   <br>
> of <br>
> an only child <br>
> John Christian Edwards <br>
> Died July 21^st^ 1873 <br>
> Aged 4 years & 7 months <br>
