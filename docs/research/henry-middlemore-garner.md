# Henry Middlemore Garner

**Died 26 April 1865**

## Henry Middlemore Garner <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of Henry Middlemore Garner's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/henry-middlemore-garner-headstone-2024.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Henry Middlemore Garner's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2024.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 26 April 1865
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. M. Garner
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2024
