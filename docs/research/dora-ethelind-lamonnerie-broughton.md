# Dora Ethelind Lamonnerie Broughton


**Died 23 May 1864**

## Dora Ethelind Lamonnerie Broughton <small>([Toowong Cemetery Symbolism Display](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A))</small>

### Headstone

![Dora Ethelind Lamonnerie Broughton's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/dora-ethelind-lamonnerie-broughton-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width"  }

*<small>A fragment of Dora Ethelind Lamonnerie Broughton's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in ==2015==.</small>*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 23 May 1864
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Dora
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1934
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, ==Portion 29A near Section 72==. ca. ==May 2015==

### Inscription

> Dora <br>
> 23d May 1864 <br>
