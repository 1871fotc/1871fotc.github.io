# Harry Dobbin Shepperson

**4 December 1868 —  17 September 1870**

## Harry Dobbin Shepperson <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Harry Dobbin's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/harry-dobbin-shepperson-headstone-2015.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

![A fragment of Harry Dobbin's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/frederick-shepperson-headstone-2015.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Harry Dobbin Shepperson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 17 September 1870
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. D. Shepperson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery  Symbology Display. ca. 2016

### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of <br>
> Harry Dobbin <br>
> the beloved son of <br>
> Harry & Mary Shepperson <br>
> born 4^th^ December 1868 <br>
> died 17…  <br>

 <br>
 
> also Frederick Shepperson  <br>
> born 7^th^ April 1872 <br>
> died 8^th^ April 1872 <br>
