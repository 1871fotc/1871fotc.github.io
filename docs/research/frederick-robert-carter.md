## Frederick Robert Carter <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="frederick-robert-carter-sharpe-headstone-2012.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of Frederick Robert Carter's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 26 June 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds,  Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as F. R. C. Sharpe
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Sacred  <br>
> To <br>
> The Memory Of <br>
> Frederick Robert Carter <br>
> Only Son Of <br>
> Mrs Edward Sharpe <br>
> Who Died June 25^th^ 1872 <br>
> Aged 23 Years <br>
>
> Absent from the body but present with the Lord

> FUNERAL.-The Friends of Mr. SHARPE are respectfully invited to attend the Funeral of his deceased Step-son, FREDERICK CARTER; the procession to move from his residence, Skinner-street, Gregory-terrace, THIS DAY, at half-past 3 o'clock, p m. 6257 F. MURRAY, Undertaker, Queen-street.


> I. O. O. F. M. U. THE OFFICERS and BROTHERS of the PRINCE OF WALES LODGE are requested to attend the FUNERAL of late Bro. F. CARTER, to move at half-past 3 o'clock THIS (Wednesday) AFTERNOON, from Skinner-street, Gregory-terrace (near the Rev. B, G. Wilson's). Officers and Brethren of sister Lodges are respectfully invited to attend. By order of the N.G., 6265 GEO. WRIDE, Secretary.
