# Alfred Compigne

**ca. 1846 — 10 May 1864**

## Alfred Compigne <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Alfred Compigne's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/alfred-compigne-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width"  }

*<small>A fragment of Alfred Compigne's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in ==20??==.</small>*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 10 May 1864
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Alfred Compigne
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1934
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May ==20??== 

### Inscription

> To the memory of  <br>
> Alfred Compigne Esq.   <br>

> Alfred Compigne Esq.   <br>
> of  <br>

> Who died on the 10th May 1864  <br>
> At Brisbane in his 18^th^ year   <br>
