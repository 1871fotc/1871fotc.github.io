# William Betz

**3 June 1868 — 26 February 1870**

## William Betz <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![William Betz's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/william-betz-headstone-2021.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of William Betz's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 26 February 1870
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as William Betz
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012
