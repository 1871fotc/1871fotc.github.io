# Joseph Matthews

**ca. 1850 — 10 March 1873**

## Joseph Matthews <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Joseph Matthews' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/joseph-matthews-headstone-2012.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

*A fragment of Joseph Matthews' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 10 March 1873
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. Matthews
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Joseph Mathews  <br>
> Late Carpenters Mate of the  <br>
> Ship Winifred <br>
> Who was accidentally killed by  <br>
> the slipping of a spar  <br>
> on the 10^th^ March 1873 <br>
> Aged XXIII years <br>
>
> … <br>
