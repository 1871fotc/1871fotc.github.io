# Caroline Jane Blakeney

**ca. 3 March 1863 — 23 March 1866**

## Caroline Jane Blakeney <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Caroline Jane Blakeney's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/caroline-jane-blakeney-headstone-2016.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Caroline Jane Blakeney's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2016.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 23 March 1866
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as C. J. Blakeney
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2016

> Caroline Jane Blakeney, buried on March 23, 1866, was a little girl, six years and 20 days, of age, daughter of William and Eliza Blakeney. Blakeney was the once well-known Registrar-General, and son of Judge Blakeney. One of his daughters married T. H. B. Barron, and another married S. B. Leishman, the squatter. Both were fine looking women. One of Mrs. Barron's daughters is the wife of one of Sir Arthur Palmer's sons. C.J. Blakeney, a once well-known lawyer at Brisbane, Cairns, and Cooktown, was another son of the Judge.
