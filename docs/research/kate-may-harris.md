# Kate May Harris 

**16 May 1862 — 19 August 1862**

## Kate May Harris <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

<small>*Kate May Harris' headstone  discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 19 August 1862
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as K. M. Harris
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Sacred to the memory of <br>
> Kate May Harris <br>
> daughter of  <br> 
> George & Jane Harris  <br>
> born May 16^th^ 1862￼ <br>
> ￼died August 19^th^ <br>
> 1862 ￼ <br>
> Jesus called … into him <br>

<br>

> …   <br>
> Sarah Emily  <br>
> … wife of <br>
> Mr. John Harris  <br>
