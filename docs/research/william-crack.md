# William Crack

#foundHeadstones #done #checkInscription

**ca. 1856 — 27 August 1874**

## William Crack <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="william-crack-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*Fragments of William Crack's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2013.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds. After 27 August 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as William Crack
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2013

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to
> the memory of <br>
> William Crack <br>
> eldest son of <br>
> William & Ellen C… <br>
> Died 27^th^ August 1874  <br>
> Aged 18 years & 6 months<br>
> 
> My days were few on earth but like <br> 
> a flower did pass away. Therefore <br>
> … I hope this life … <br>
> …  not be part <br>
