# Julius William Deedes

**ca. 1831 — 3 November 1866**

## Julius William Deedes <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![Julius William Deedes' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/julius-william-deedes-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Julius William Deedes' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 3 November 1866
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. W. Deedes
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery Symbolism Display. ca. 2016


### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory of <br>
> Julius William Deedes <br>
> who died  <br>
> November 3^rd^ 1866 <br>
> aged 35 years <br>
