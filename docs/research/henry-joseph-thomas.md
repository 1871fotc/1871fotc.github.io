# Henry Joseph Thomas

**ca. 1811 — 4 July 1865**

## Henry Joseph Thomas <small>([Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of Henry Joseph Thomas' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/henry-joseph-thomas-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragment of Henry Joseph Thomas' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 4 July 1865
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. J. Thomas, Captain
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery Symbolism Display. ca. 2016

- [TELEGRAPHIC. (1865, July 5). The Brisbane Courier (Qld. : 1864 - 1933), p. 3.](https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/1274801/52158#)

> Yesterday evening, an inquest on the body of Mr. H. J. Thomas, better known as Captain Thomas, was held at Fairfield-house. The deceased died suddenly, from the effects, it is stated, of a dose of arsenic. The evidence adduced showed that the deceased had been for some time past suffering very much from mental anxiety, which lately caused him to take to drink. He went out on Monday night, apparently in his usual health, and returned about 4 a.m. yesterday the worse of liquor, and went to bed. About 8 o'clock he got up, and went out. On his return, Mrs. Thomas tried to induce him to take some breakfast, and after wards made him go again to bed. He then had some conversation with the servant girl, during which he swallowed something which he stated was arsenic. In a short time he became very ill, and Drs. Bell and Lansdown were called in, but, notwithstanding all that medical skill could devise, he died about 2 o'clock yesterday afternoon. The inquest will be resumed at 2 o'clock this afternoon, it having been adjourned for the purpose of obtaining further medical testimony.
