# John Wilson

#foundHeadstones

**ca. 1846 — 7 August 1872**

## John Wilson <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="john-wilson-headstone.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of John Wilson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2016*</small>


- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 7 August 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as John Wilson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2016

### Inscription

> In <br>
> memory <br>
> of <br>
> John Wilson <br>
> (Constable) <br>
> who died at Brisbane  <br>
> 7^th^ August 1872 <br>
> aged 26 years <br>
