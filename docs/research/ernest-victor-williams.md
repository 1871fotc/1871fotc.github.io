# Ernest Victor Williams

**ca. 30 May 1868 — 20 February 1869**

## Ernest Victor Williams <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![Ernest Victor Williams's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/ernest-victor-williams-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Ernest Victor Williams's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After ==died d mmmm yyyy==
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. V. Williams
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015

### Inscription

> In <br>
> Memory of <br>
> Ernest Victor <br>
> The beloved child of <br>
> Samuel & Georgiana  <br>
> Adele Williams <br>
> Who died <br>
> February 20^th^ 1869 <br>
> Aged eight months & three weeks <br>
> Of such is the kingdom of heaven <br>
