# William Hugh Morris

**ca. 1840 — 17 September 1866**

## William Hugh Morris <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of William Hugh Morris' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/william-hugh-morris-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of William Hugh Morris' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 17 September 1866
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as William Morriss	
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> In memory of  <br>
> William Morris  <br>
> late of Penisarwaen  <br>
> Arvon, North Wales  <br>
> Died Sep^t^ 17^th^ 1866. <br>
> …ged 26 years  <br>
