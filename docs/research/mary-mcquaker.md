<small>*Fragments of Mary McQuaker's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 13 August 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Mary McQuaker
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023
