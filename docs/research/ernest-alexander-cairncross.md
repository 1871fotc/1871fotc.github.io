# Ernest Alexander Cairncross

**28 August 1867 — 20 September 1867**

## Ernest Alexander Cairncross <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of Ernest Alexander Cairncross' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/ernest-alexander-cairncross-headstone-2015.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragment of Ernest Alexander Cairncross' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 20 September 1867
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Ernest A. Cairncross
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery Symbolism Display. ca. 2016


### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of <br>
> Earnest Alexander  <br>
> Cairncross <br>
> died 20 of September 1867 <br>
> aged 21 days <br>
> So God blessed Baby <br>
