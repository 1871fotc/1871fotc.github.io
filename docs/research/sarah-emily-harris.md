# Sarah Emily Harris

**ca. 1787 — 17 September 1866** 

## Sarah Emily Harris <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

<small>*Fragments of Sarah Emily Harris' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 17 September 1866 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as S. E. Harris
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

> Sarah Emily Harris, who died on September 17, 1866, aged 78, was the mother of John and George Harris, once the leading Brisbane merchants and shipping agents.
> 
> George married a sister of the late George Thorn, of Ipswich and their well-known home, "Newstead," at the mouth of Breakfast Creek, was famous for its generous hospitallity.
