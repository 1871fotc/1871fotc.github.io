## Clara Ann Hopkins <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="clara-ann-hopkins-headstone-2021.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of Clara Ann Hopkins' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2016.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 12 April 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as C. A. Hopkins
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2016

> DEATH. <br>

> Can anyone enlighten us concerning Clara Ann Hopkins, who died on April 12, 1874, aged 29, and on whose grave is this extraordinary verse — <br> <br>
> She is not as we saw her last, On a suffering dying bed ; <br>
> To her all death and pain are past, And by living streams she is led  <br>
> She has learned the sacred story, Of the Saviour's dying love. <br>
> Her eyes now see the glory That awaited her above."  <br> <br>
> If the writer of this had seen the look in the eyes of those who read it, he would <br>
> HAVE FLED AWAY SOMEWHERE <br>
