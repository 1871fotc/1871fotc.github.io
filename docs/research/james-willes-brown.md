# James Willes Brown

**Died 18 March 1866**

## James Willes Brown <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![James Willes Brown's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/james-willes-brown-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Base of James Willes Brown's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 18 March 1866
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. W. Brown
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> … <br>
> the loving memory <br>
> of <br>
> James Willes Brown <br>
> son of  <br>
