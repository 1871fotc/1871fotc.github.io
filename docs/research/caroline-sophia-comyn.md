# Caroline Sophia Comyn

**ca. 1842 — 16 September 1869**

## Caroline Sophia Comyn <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![Caroline Sophia Comyn's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/caroline-sophia-comyn-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Caroline Sophia Comyn's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 16 September 1869
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as C. S. C. Vallack
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display. ca. 2016


### Inscription

> In loving memory <br>
> of <br>
> Caroline Sophia Comyn￼￼  <br>
> eldest daughter of <br>
> William & Lucy Vallack <br>
> of Balmain Sydney N.S.W. <br>
> Died 16 of September 1869 <br>
> Aged 27 years <br>
