# Edgar Porter


**27 October 1863 — 27 November 1863**

## Edgar Porter <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Edgar Porter's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/porter-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Edgar Porter's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 27 November 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. Porter
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to memory of <br>
> Herbert & Edgar  <br>
> twin sons  of <br> 
> Nicholas & Rebecca Porter <br>
> who died in infancy <br>
