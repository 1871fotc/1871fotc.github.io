# John Parker

**ca. 1823 — 15 March 1872**

## John Parker <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of John Parker's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/john-parker-headstone-2013.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

![Fragment of John Parker's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/thomas-parker-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of John Parker's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2013 (Top) and 2014.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 15 March 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as John Parker
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2013 and May 2014

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to <br>
> the memory of   <br>
> John Parker <br>
> of Featherstone Yorkshire  <br>
> who departed this life <br>
> 15^th^ March 1872 <br>
> aged 49 years.  <br>
> … his son <br>

<br>

> Thomas Parker
