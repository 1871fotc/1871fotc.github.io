# Thomas Priestley Mitchison

**ca. 1830 — 14 April 1858**

## Thomas Priestley Mitchison <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of Thomas Priestley Mitchison's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/thomas-priestley-mitchinson-headstone-2012-2.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Thomas Priestley Mitchison's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 14 April 1858
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Thos. P. Mitchinson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> In memory of   <br>
> Thomas Priestley Mitchinson E.  <br>
> late of Ballarat Victoria <br>
> who departed this life  <br>
> 14^th^ April A.D. 1858 <br>
> Aged 28 years <br>
