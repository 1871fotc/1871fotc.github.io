# Betsy Mather

**ca. 1833 — 1 February 1863**

## Betsy Mather <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Betsy Mather's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/betsy-mather-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Betsy Mather's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 1 February 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as B. M. Mather
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred  <br>
> to the memory of  <br>
> Betsey the beloved wife of <br>
> Robert Mather  <br>
> who departed this life  <br>
> Feb 2 1863 aged 30 years <br>
