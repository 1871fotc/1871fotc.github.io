# Richard Bower

**ca. 1832 — 12 July 1866**

## Richard Bower <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Richard Bower's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/richard-bower-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Richard Bower's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 12 July 1866
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Richard Bower
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Sacred  <br>
> to <br>
> the memory of <br>
> Richard Bower <br>
> who departed this life <br>
> 12^th^ July 1866 <br>
> Aged 34 years <br>
>
> I am the resurrection, and the life: he <br>
> that believeth in me, though he were <br>
> dead, yet shall he live <br>
