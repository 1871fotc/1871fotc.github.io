# Richard Stanley Mosse

**ca. 1852 — 7 May 1872**

##  Richard Stanley Mosse <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Richard Stanley Mosse's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/richard-stanley-mosse-headstone-2014.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*Richard Stanley Mosse's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. ca. 7 May 1872
- May have been moved to North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. There is a record for R.S. Morse.
- May have been disposed of in Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to <br>
> the memory <br>
> of <br>
> Richard Stanley Mosse <br>
> Son of the Late <br>
> Richard Mosse Esq. <br>
> Bennett's Bridge <br>
> County Kilkenny Ireland <br>
> Who departed this life <br>
> on the 7^th^ day of May <br>
> A.D. 1872 <br>
> ÆTAT 20 Years <br> <br>
> Even so them also which <br>
> sleep in Jesus will God <br>
> bring with him. 1 Thess iv. xiv <br>
