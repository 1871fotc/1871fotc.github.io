# George Hutchison

**ca. 1828 — 7 June 1872**

## George Hutchison <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of George Hutchison's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/george-hutchison-headstone-2024.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*A fragment of George Hutchison's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2024.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 7 June 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as George Hutchinson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2024

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to <br>
> the memory of  <br>
> George Hutchinson  <br>
> who departed this life <br>
> June 6^th^ 1872 <br>
> aged 44 years <br>
