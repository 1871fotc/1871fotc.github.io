# George Thompson

**ca. 1829 — 26 October 1871**

## George Thompson <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of George Thompson's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/george-thompson-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

*A fragment of George Thompson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2024.*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 26 October 1871
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as George Thompson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2024

### Inscription

> George Thompson   <br>
> Died 26^th^ October  1871  <br>
> Aged 43 …ears  <br>
