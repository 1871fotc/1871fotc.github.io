# Elizabeth Jane Venner

**Died 24 May 1872**

## Elizabeth Jane Venner ([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))

### Headstone

![A fragment of Elizabeth Jane Venner's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/elizabeth-jane-venner-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Elizabeth Jane Venner's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds. After 24 May 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. J. Verner
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> also of 
> Elizabeth Jane <br>
> …elict of the above  <br>
> …o died May 24^th^ 1872 <br>
