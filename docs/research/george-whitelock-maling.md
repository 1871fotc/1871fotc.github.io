# George Whitelock Maling

#foundHeadstones  #newPhoto

**20 May 1871 — 23 December 1871**

## George Whitelock Maling <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="george-whitelock-maling-headstone-2024.jpg" width="50%" /> 

<small>*The base of George Whitelock Maling's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs in 2024.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 23 December 1871
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as George W.	Mating
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2024

### Inscription

> Sacred  <br>
> to the memory of  <br>
> George Whitelock  <br>
> infant son of   <br>
> Irwin C. Maling   <br>
> Born at Sea 20^th^ May 1871 <br>
> Died at Brisbane 23^rd^ Dec 1871 <br>

> A child's grave bears the name of Irwin Maling, who was a military captain connected with a <br>
> DETACHMENT OF THE 50TH REGIMENT <br>
> which bore the name of "the dirty half-hundred,” a name said to have been acquired by their severe economies in personal expenditure, especially where ladies were concerned.

- [BYGONE BRISBANE. (1908, February 16). Truth (Brisbane, Qld. : 1900 - 1954), p. 7.](https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/206486987?searchTerm=maling)

> We omitted to mention that Irwin Maling, of the last chapter, was the Captain Maling who was private secretary to Lord Normanby. He was brother-in-law of General English, of the 53rd. Regiment, the "Shropshire Dashers." English married Maling's sister.
