# Herbert Porter

#foundHeadstones

**27 October 1863 — 17 November 1863**

## Herbert Porter <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="porter-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of Herbert Porter's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 17 November 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. Porter
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to memory of <br>
> Herbert & Edgar  <br>
> twin sons  of <br> 
> Nicholas & Rebecca Porter <br>
> who died in infancy <br>
