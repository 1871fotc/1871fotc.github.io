# Henry Arthur Herbert

**4 February 1869 — 25 April 1872** 

## Henry Arthur Herbert <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Henry Arthur Herbert's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/henry-arthur-herbert-headstone-2012.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Henry Arthur Herbert's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds , Episcopalian section. After 25 April 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. A. Herbert
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Sacred  <br>
> to the memory of  <br>
