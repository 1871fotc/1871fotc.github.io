# Richard Henry Watson

#foundHeadstones

**ca. 1807 — 12 July 1868** 

## Richard Henry Watson <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="richard-henry-watson-headstone-2014.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*Richard Henry Watson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 12 July 1868 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as R. H. Watson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014

### Inscription

> Sacred  <br>
> to <br>
> the memory of <br>
> Richard H. Watson  <br>
> who died <br>
> July 12^th^ 1868 <br>
> aged 61 years  <br>
