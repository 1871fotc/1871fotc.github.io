### Headstone

![A fragment of John Sewell's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/john-sewell-headstone-2014.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*A fragment of John Sewell's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds.  After ==31 July 1865==
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014
