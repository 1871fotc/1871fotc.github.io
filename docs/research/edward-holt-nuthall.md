# Edward Holt Nuthall

**ca. 1829 — 21 September 1860**

## Edward Holt Nuthall ([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))

### Headstone

![Edward Holt Nuthall's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/edward-holt-nuthall-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Edward Holt Nuthall's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 21 September 1860
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. H. Nuttall
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory of   <br>
> Edward Holt Nuthall  <br>
> A native of England <br>
> … died September 21^st^ 1860<br>
> from consumption <br>
> Aged 31 years  <br>
