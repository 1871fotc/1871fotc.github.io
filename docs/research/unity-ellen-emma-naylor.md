### Headstone

<small>*A fragment of Unity Naylor's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 28 February 1873
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Unity E. E. Naylor
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014
