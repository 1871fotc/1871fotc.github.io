# John Proud

**==ca. 1819== — 14 July 1872**

## John Proud <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of John Proud's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/john-proud-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*A fragment of John Proud's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 14 July 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as John Proud
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to <br>
> the memory of <br>
> John Proud <br>
> who departed this life  <br>
> July 14^th^ 1872 <br>
> aged ==53== years <br>
> Thy will be done <br>
> …ected by his beloved  <br>
> widow <br>
