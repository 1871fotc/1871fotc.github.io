# Sarah Williams

#foundHeadstones 

**ca. 1797 — 7 April 1849**

## Sarah Williams <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="sarah-williams-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" />

*Fragments of Sarah William's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 7 April 1849 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Sarah Williams
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred
> to the memory of  <br>
> of  <br>
> M^rs^ Sarah Williams  <br>
> the beloved wife of  <br>
> M^r^ John Williams <br>
> of South Brisbane  <br>
> who after a long and painful <br>
> illness  which she bore with Christi…   <br>
