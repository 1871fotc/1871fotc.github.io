# Margaret Laing

**16 November 1861 — 6 February 1863**

## Margaret Laing <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of Margaret Laing's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/laing-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Margaret Laing's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs in 2021.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section.  After 6 February 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as M. Laing
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2021

- [BYGONE BRISBANE. (1907, December 8). Truth (Brisbane, Qld. : 1900 - 1954), p. 10.](https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/206482730#)

> Next to him is a grave with four children named Laing, four little girls, Helen, Margaret, Ann and Elizabeth, aged 11, 13, 14, and 17 months, not one reaching two years of age. Three died in 1863, and one in 1873, so the first three must be the children of two mothers, unless two were twin. A cypress pine, "Callitris Robusta," evidently an old tree, has fallen between the two graves, and lies partly on the stone over Mathieson, with a branch over the little girls. The four dead children, the dead man, and the dead cypress ! There is no more pathetic or mournful scene in the cemetery.
