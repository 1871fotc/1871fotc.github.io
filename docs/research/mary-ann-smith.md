# Mary Ann Smith

**28 May 1865 — 16 June 1865**

## Mary Ann Smith <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Mary Ann Smith's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/walter-bushell-smith-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Mary Ann Smith's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 16 June 1865
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as M. A. Smith
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory <br>
> of  <br>
> Walter Bushell  <br>
> son of <br>
> George & Jane Smith <br>
> and grandson of   <br>
> Walter Bushell Smith  <br>
> Bricklayer,  <br>
> of Liverpool England <br>
> who died March 1^st^ 1865  <br>
> aged 5 years.  <br>
> <br>
> also Mary Ann, <br>
> daughter of the above <br>
> aged 19 days  <br>
> Suffer little children to  come  <br>
> unto me   <br>
