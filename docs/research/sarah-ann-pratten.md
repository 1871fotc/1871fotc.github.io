# Sarah Ann Pratten

**ca. 1837 — 21 November 1859**

## Sarah Ann Pratten <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone and Footstone

![Sarah Ann Pratten's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/sarah-ann-pratten-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

![Sarah Ann Pratten's footstone](../../assets/dig-finds/sarah-ann-pratten-footstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*Sarah Ann Pratten's headstone and footstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 21 November 1859
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Sarah Ann Pratten
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2023

### Inscription

#### Headstone

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory of  <br>
> Sarah Ann Pratten <br>
> Who departed this Life <br>
> 21^st^ Nov 1859 <br>
> aged 23 years  <br>
> <br>
> She is not dead but sleepeth <br>

#### Footstone

> S.A.P. <br>
> 1859 <br>

- [BYGONE BRISBANE. (1908, March 8). Truth (Brisbane, Qld. : 1900 - 1954), p. 7.](https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/206481341?searchTerm=pratten)

> A girl named Sarah Ann Pratten died in 1859, aged 23 the age— from 23 to 26 — fatal to so many of the young women in the early days, Miss Pratten was an aunt of F. L. Pratten, the present Deputy Registrar of Titles in Brisbane. Her father, the granddad of the present Prattens, came to Brisbane in the forties, and was farming at Cowper's Plains, to-day erroniously called "Cooper's Plains;" though named after Dr. Cowper, the first medical officer in the convict settlement at Moreton Bay. Pratten senior died at the Plains, and was buried there.
