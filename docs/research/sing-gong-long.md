# Sing Gong Long

## Sing Gong Long <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of Sing Gong Long's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/sing-gong-long-headstone-2026.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Sing Gong Long's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Long Sing Gong
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. 

### Inscription 

> In memory <br>
> of <br>
> Sing Gong Long <br>
> … <br>
