# Reuben Oliver

#foundHeadstones #done

**4 May 1867 — 21 September 1871**

## Reuben Oliver <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="reuben-oliver-headstone-2014.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*Reuben Oliver's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 21 September 1871
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as R. Oliver
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory of  <br>
> Reuben, <br>
> son of <br>
> Reuben & Ann Oliver
> who died <br>
> September 21^st^ 1871<br>
> Aged 4 years  <br>
