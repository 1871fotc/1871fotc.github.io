# James Robert Balfour 

**ca. 1821 — 13 March 1844**

## James Robert Balfour  <small>([Toowong Cemetery Museum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JaMz9L7Np7mRW92w6))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of James Robert Balfour's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/james-robert-balfour-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of James Robert Balfour's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 13 March 1844
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as James Balfour
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014

### Inscription

> In Memory <br>
> of <br>
> …ames R Balf…  <br>
> … of Colinton …  <br>
> …o departed th…  <br>
> ==12^th^==  March 18…  <br>
> aged 23 year…  <br>
