# James Joseph Fitzgerald

**Died 19 April 1867**

## James Joseph Fitzgerald <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of James Joseph Fitzgerald's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/james-joseph-fitzgerald-headstone-2026.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragment of James Joseph Fitzgerald's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 19 April 1867
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. J. Fitzgerald
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014
