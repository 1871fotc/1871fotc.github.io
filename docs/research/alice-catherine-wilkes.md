# Alice Catherine Wilkes

**4 November 1847 — 23 February 1853**

## Alice Catherine Wilkes <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Alice Catherine Wilkes's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/alice-catherine-wilkes-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Alice Catherine Wilkes's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 23 February 1853 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Alice Catherine Wilkes
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

> DIED. <br>
> At South Brisbane, on the 23rd instant, after a short but severe illness, which she endured with the most exemplary meekness and fortitude, Alice Catherine, eldest daughter of Mr. William Wilkes, aged five years, three months, and nineteen days. Her natural intelligence and amiability of disposition had gained her the love of all who knew her in life, and her early death has inspired feelings of the most bitter regret.
