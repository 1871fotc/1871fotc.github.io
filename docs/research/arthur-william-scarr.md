## Arthur William Scarr <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="scarr-headstone-2012.jpg" width="50%" /> 

<small>*Fragments of Arthur William Scarr's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs in 2012.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 4 December 1869  
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as A. W. Scarr
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012
