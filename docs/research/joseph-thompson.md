# Joseph Thompson

#foundHeadstones

**ca. 1819 — 19 December 1857**

## Joseph Thompson <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="joseph-thompson-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" />

*A fragment of Joseph Thompson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 19 December 1857
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Joseph Thompson 
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> In Memory  <br>
> of   <br>
> Joseph Thompson <br>
> who   <br>
> died 19^th^ Dec^r^ 1857  <br>
> aged 38 years <br>
