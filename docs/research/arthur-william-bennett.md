# Arthur William Bennett


**21 November 1866 — 9 January 1868**

## Arthur William Bennett <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

<!-- move to /dig-finds -->

![Arthur William Bennett's headstone](../../assets/arthur-william-bennett-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Arthur William Bennett's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012*</small>


- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 9 January 1868
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as A. W. Bennett and infant
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Arthur William <br>
> Born November 21^st^ 1866 <br>
> Died January 9^th^ 1868. <br>
> And an infant son
