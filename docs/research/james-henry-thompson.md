# James Henry Thompson

#foundHeadstones

**ca. 1841 — 9 June 1869**

## James Henry Thompson <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="james-henry-thompson-headstone-2012.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*Fragments of James Henry Thompson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 9 June 1869
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. H.	Thompson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> To the memor…  <br>
> James Henry Thomson <br>
> of this city  <br>
> who died June 9^th^ …   <br>
> aged  28 years <br> 
> also of  <br>
> Emily Matilda <br>
> infant daughter of the above <br>
> who die… April 11^th^ 1867  <br>
> aged … and 2 weeks <br>
