# John Woodward

**ca. 1829 — 12 April 1864**

### Learn more 


- [TELEGRAPHIC NEWS. (1864, April 11). The Brisbane Courier (Qld. : 1864 - 1933), p. 2.](https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/1257824?searchTerm=woodward#)

> An accident of a serious nature occurred on Saturday afternoon to Mr. Woodward, book-keeper, at Mr. Hardie's station, at Fassifern. It appears that the unfortunate gentleman started from South Brisbane, in company with Mr. Hardie, the Rev. Mr. Rumsey, and Mr. Howe, superintendent at Fassifern, about four o'clock, intending to reach Ipswich before dark. They had proceeded some five or six miles on their way, riding at a very rapid pace, when Mr. Woodward's horse (a very spirited animal) got quite beyond its rider's control, and dashed full against a dray coming in the opposite direction, unseating Mr. Woodward bringing him in contact with the iron of the dray, and breaking his leg in three places. He was placed on the dray, and conveyed as quickly as was compatible with safely into South Brisbane, where he was immediately attended by Dr. Lyons, who, on examination, found that he had sustained a compound fracture of the thigh, and laceration of the femoral artery. Temporary remedies having been applied he was taken to the hospital, where it was at first thought amputation was absolutely necessary. Subsequently, however, that idea was abandoned, and last evening the patient was progressing favorably.
