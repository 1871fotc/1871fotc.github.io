# Elizabeth Hill

**Died 13 June 1860**

## Elizabeth Hill ([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))

### Headstone

![A fragment of Elizabeth Hill's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/elizabeth-hill-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Elizabeth Hill's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2021.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 13 June 1860
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. Hill
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2021
