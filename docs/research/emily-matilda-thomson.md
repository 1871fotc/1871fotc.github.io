# Emily Matilda Thomson

**ca. January 1867 — 11 April 1867**

## Emily Matilda Thomson <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499)
)</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of Emily Matilda Thomson's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/james-henry-thompson-headstone-2012.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Emily Matilda Thomson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 11 April 1867
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. M. Thompson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> To the memor…  <br>
> James Henry Thomson <br> 
> of this city  <br>
> who died June 9^th^ …   <br>
> aged  28 years <br> 
> also of  <br>
> Emily Matilda <br>
> infant daughter of the above <br>
> who die… April 11^th^ 1867  <br>
> aged … and 2 weeks <br>
