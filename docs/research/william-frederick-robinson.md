### Headstone

<small>*Fragments of William Frederick Robinson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 17 September 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as William Frederick Robinson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023
