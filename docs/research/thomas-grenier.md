# Thomas Grenier

**ca. 1840 — 25 August 1857**

## Thomas Grenier <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Thomas Grenier's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/thomas-grenier-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*Thomas Grenier's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 25 August 1857
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Thomas Grenier
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> In Memory  <br>
> of  <br>
> Thomas  <br>
> eldest son of  <br>
> Thomas and Mary Grenier <br>
> of South Brisbane  <br>
> who died Aug^t^ 25^th^ 1857 <br>
> aged 17 years  <br>
