# John Brougham Copeland

#foundHeadstones #toDoHeadstones #index #indexInPDF #symbology

**ca. 1833 — 19 April 1867**

## John Brougham Copeland <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="john-brougham-copeland-headstone-2015.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*Fragment of John Brougham Copeland's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 19 April 1867
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. B. Copeland
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery Symbolism Display. ca. 2016


### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory <br>
> of <br>
> John Brougham Copeland <br>
> Late of Sheffield England <br>
> who died <br>
> April 19^th^ 1867 <br>
> aged 34 years <br>
