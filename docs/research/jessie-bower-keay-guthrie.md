## Jessie Bower Keay Guthrie <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<small>*A fragment of Jessie Bower Keay Guthrie's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 20 June 1871 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Jessie B. K.	Guthrie
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012
