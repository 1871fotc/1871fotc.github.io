# Edward Rice Thomas

**ca. 1821 — 31 July 1853**

## Edward Rice Thomas ([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))

### Headstone

![A fragment of Edward Rice Thomas' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/edward-rice-thomas-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }


<small>*A fragment of Edward Rice Thomas' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 31 July 1853
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as E. R. Thomas
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023
- Moved to: Family in Tasmania ca. 2023

### Inscription

> Beneath this stone <br>
> Lie the mortal remains of   <br>
> Edward Rice Thomas  <br>
> fourth son of <br>
> Jocelyn Thomas Esq.<br>
> of North Down house <br>
> Van Diemens Land <br>
> who departed this life <br>
> on the 31^st^ July A.D. 1853 <br>
> ÆTATIS 32 years  <br>
> … <br>
