# John Alpin Webster

**Died 24 June 1871**

## John Alpin Webster <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of John Alpin Webster's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/james-william-and-john-aplin-webster-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

*Fragments of John Alpin Webster's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.*

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 24 June 1871
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. A. Webster
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015

### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of  <br>
> James William Webster  <br>
> late … <br>
> at Cleveland  <br>
> Who died at Brisbane   <br>
> February 22^nd^ 1873  <br>
> aged 32 years  <br>

  <br>
  
> And  <br>
> his infant son  <br>
> …own Aplin Webs…   <br>
> Who died June 24^th^ …  <br>
