## Mary Warburton <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="mary-warburton-headstone-2012.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of Mary Warburton's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 16 August 1865 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Mary Warburton
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Sacred
> to the memory of  <br>
> Mary the beloved wife <br>
> … nd Warburton <br>
