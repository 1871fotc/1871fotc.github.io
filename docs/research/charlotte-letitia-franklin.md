- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds. After 16 July 1867
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as C. L. Franklin
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012 and 22 May 2021
