# Thomas Ritchie

**9 April 1870 — 14 May 1875**

## Thomas Ritchie <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Thomas Ritchie's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/eliza-jane-and-thomas-ritchie-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Thomas Ritchie's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 14 May 1875
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as T. Ritchie
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred
> to the memory of <br>
> Eliza Jane Ritchie  <br>
> who died February 22^nd^ 1869 <br>
> aged 5 months <br>
> also her brother <br>
> Thomas Ritchie <br>
> who died May 14^th^ 1875 <br>
> aged 5 years <br>
> of such is the kingdom of heaven ￼
