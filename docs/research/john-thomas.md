# John Thomas

**ca. 1824 — 25 September 1864**

## John Thomas <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of John Thomas' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/john-thomas-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of John Thomas' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 25 September 1864
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as John Thomas
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> Sacred
> to the mem…   <br>
> John Tho… <br>
> who  departed this…  <br>
> September 25^th^ 18…  <br>
> aged 40 years <br>
