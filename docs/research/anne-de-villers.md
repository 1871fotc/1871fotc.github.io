# Anne De Villers

**ca. 1804 — 30 April 1873**

## Anne De Villers <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Anne De Villers' headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/anne-de-villers-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Anne De Villers' headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2021.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 30 April 1873
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Ann De Villers
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2021

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory of <br>
> Anne De Villers <br>
> wife of  <br>
> Hamilton De Villers <br>
> who departed this life 
> 30^th^ April 1873  <br>
> Aged 69 years <br>
