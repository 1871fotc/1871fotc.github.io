# John Brakenridge

#foundHeadstones #done

**ca. 1833 — 26 March 1864**

## John Brakenridge <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="john-brakenridge-headstone-2012.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of John Brakenridge's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 26 March 1864
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. Brackenridge
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> Jesu Mercy <br>
> In Memory of <br>
> The Reverend <br>
> John Brakenridge M.A. <br>
> of Christ's College  <br>
> Cambridge <br>
> Clerk in Holy Orders <br>
