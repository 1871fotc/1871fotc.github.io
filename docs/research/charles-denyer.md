## Charles Denyer <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="charles-denyer-headstone-2026.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of Charles Denyer's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2024.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 9 January 1865
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Charles Denyer
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2024

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to the memory <br>
> of   <br>
> Charles Denyer <br>
