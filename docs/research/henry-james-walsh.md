# Henry James Walsh

**20 February 1861 — 3 April 1865**

## Henry James Walsh <small>[(Toowong Cemetery Symbology Display)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yDA2J7YJrrdirAJ4A)</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of Henry James Walsh's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/henry-james-walsh-headstone.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragment of Henry James Walsh's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2014.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 3 April 1865
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. J. Walsh
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2014
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery Symbolism Display. ca. 2016
