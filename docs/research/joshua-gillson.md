> DEATH. <br>
> GILLSON.—On the 2nd September, Joshua Gillson, aged 46 years, father of Mrs. W. Boys and Mrs T. Rooksley, of this city, a native of Fen Ditton, Cambridge, England, after undergoing a painful operation at the hospital, Bowen Bridge. Much respected and beloved by all who knew him. He leaves a wife and 4 children to mourn his loss.
