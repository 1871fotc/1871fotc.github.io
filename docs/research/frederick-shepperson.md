### Headstone

<img src="harry-dobbin-shepperson-headstone-2015.jpg" width="50%" /> 

<img src="frederick-shepperson-headstone-2015.jpg" width="50%" /> 

<small>*Fragments of Frederick Shepperson's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2015.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 8 April 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as H. D. Shepperson
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2015
- Moved to: Toowong Cemetery  Symbology Display. ca. 2016
