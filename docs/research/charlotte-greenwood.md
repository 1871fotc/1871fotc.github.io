# Charlotte Greenwood

**ca. 1832 — 16 March 1857**

## Charlotte Greenwood <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

<!-- move to /dig-finds -->

![A fragment of Charlotte Greenwood's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/charlotte-greenwood-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Charlotte Greenwood's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds. After 16 March 1857
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Charlotte Greenwood
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> In memory  <br>
> of <br>
> Charlotte <br>
> wife of <br>
> Christopher Henry Greenwood Esq. <br>
> who departed this life <br>
> 16^th^ March 1857    <br>
> aged 25 years <br>
>
> Thy will be done <br>
