# Charles Frederick Warren

#foundHeadstones  

**24 September 1862— 19 March 1863**

## Charles Frederick Warren  <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="charles-frederick-warren-headstone.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*Charles Frederick Warren's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 19 March 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as C. F. Warren
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.

### Inscription

> In <br>
> memory of  <br>
> C. F. Warren    <br>
> who departed  <br>
> this life 18^th^ March   <br>
> 1863 <br>
> aged 6 months <br>
