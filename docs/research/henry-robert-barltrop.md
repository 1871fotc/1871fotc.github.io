# Henry Robert Barltrop

**Died 20 December 1872**

## Henry Robert Barltrop <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

<!-- move to /dig-finds -->

![](../../assets/henry-robert-barltrop-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Henry Robert Barltrop's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2021.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 20 December 1872
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Henry Robert Barlthrop
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2021

### Inscription

> Sacred <br>
> to <br>
> the memory of <br>
> Henry Robert <br>
> eldest and beloved son of <br>
> Henry & Margaret <br>
> …
