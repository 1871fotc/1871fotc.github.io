## John Shepherd <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="john-shepherd-headstone-2014.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of John Shepherd's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012*</small>


- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 4 April 1871
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as J. Shepherd
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012
