# Duncan Francis Yaldwyn

**ca. March 1867 — 13 May 1867**

## Duncan Francis Yaldwyn <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragment of Duncan Francis Yaldwyn's](../../assets/dig-finds/duncan-francis-yaldwyn-headstone-2016.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragment of Duncan Francis Yaldwyn's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2016.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 13 May 1867
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as D. F. Yaldwyn
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2016

### Inscription

> In Memory of <br>
> Duncan Francis Yaldwyn <br>
> infant so of <br>
> William Yaldwyn Esq. <br>
> who died <br>
> on 13^th^ May 1867 <br>
