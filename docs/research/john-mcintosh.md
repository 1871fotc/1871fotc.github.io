# John McIntosh

**Died 21 May 1863**

## John McIntosh <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of John McIntosh's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/john-mcintosh-headstone-2026.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of John McIntosh's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Presbyterian section. After 21 May 1863
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as John McIntosh
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012
