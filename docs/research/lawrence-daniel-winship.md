# Lawrence Daniel Winship

**24 June 1862 — 4 July 1862**

## Lawrence Daniel Winship <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Fragments of Lawrence Daniel Winship's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/walter-brisbane-winship-2012.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Lawrence Daniel Winship's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 4 July 1862
- Not recorded as being moved to the North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2012

### Inscription

> This stone <br>
> marks the spot <br>
> where the earthly <br>
> remains of <br>
> Walter Brisbane Winship <br>
> are Interred <br>
> He died <br>
> ==in Brisbane ?== <br>
> ==on the 21^st^ of May 1862== <br>
> ==Aged 8 years and 9 months== <br>
> ==Also== <br>
> ==? Daniel Winship== <br>
> ==Infant son== <br>
> ==… 1862== <br>
