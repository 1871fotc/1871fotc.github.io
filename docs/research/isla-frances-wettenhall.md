# Isla Frances Wettenhall

**31 March 1864 — 28 April 1864**

## Isla Frances Wettenhall <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![A fragment of Isla Frances Wettenhall's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/wettenhall-headstone-2023.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*A fragment of Isla Frances Wettenhall's headstone discovered in Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Digs.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 28 April 1864
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as I. F. A. Wettenhall
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.

### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of  <br>
> Isla Frances    <br>
> daughter of  <br>
> Allan Ravenscroft   <br>
> and <br>
> Sarah Anne Wettenhall <br>
> born 31^st^ March 1864 <br>
> died 28^th^ April 1864 <br>
> Also <br>
> Allan Ravenscroft <br>
> son of the above <br>
> born 16^th^ November 1867 <br>
> died 30^th^ June 1871 <br>
> Also <br>
> Stanhope Holford <br>
> son of the above <br>
> born 22^nd^ June 1872 <br>
> died 11^th^ April 1874 <br>
