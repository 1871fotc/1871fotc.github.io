# William Henry Roberts

**15 September 1828 — 15 November 1831**

--8<-- "snippets/william-henry-roberts.md"

### Inscription 

> Sacred to the memory of William Henry, son of Charles Roberts, Commissariat Department, <br>
> departed this life 15th November, 1831, aged 5 years and 2 months. <br>
> Before my infant heart could pray <br>
> The Almighty Word was given <br>
> To stop my pain, and die away, <br>
> Then mount my soul to Heaven. <br>
> Suffer little children to come unto Me, for of such is the Kingdom of Heaven. <br>
