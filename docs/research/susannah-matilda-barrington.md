# Susannah Matilda Barrington

**ca. 1826 — 24 June 1860**

## Susannah Matilda Barrington <small>([29A](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgb8biUeEF9NnF499))</small>

### Headstone

![Susannah Matilda Barrington's headstone](../../assets/dig-finds/susannah-matilda-barrington-headstone-2012.jpg){ width="40%" class="full-width" loading=lazy }

<small>*Fragments of Susannah Matilda Barrington's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2012.*</small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 24 June 1860
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as S. M. Barrington
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72.  ca. May 2012
