# Thomas Hole

#foundHeadstones

**ca. 1819 — 7 December 1874**

## Thomas Hole <small>(29A)</small>

### Headstone

<img src="thomas-hole-headstone-2023.jpg" width="50%" />

<small>*A fragment of Thomas Hole's headstone discovered in a Toowong Cemetery Archaeological Dig in 2023.* </small>

- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds. After 7 December 1874
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Thomas Hole
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

### Inscription

> In memory <br>
> of  <br>
> Thomas Hole <br>
> Died 7^th^ December 1874 <br>
> Aged 55 years <br>
