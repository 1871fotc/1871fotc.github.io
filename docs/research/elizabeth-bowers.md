- Erected: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Episcopalian section. After 30 June 1859 
- Moved to: North Brisbane Burial Grounds, Reserved Area. ca. 1914. Recorded as Elizabeth Bowers 
- Demolished and disposed of in: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A. ca. 1930
- Discovered: Toowong Cemetery, Portion 29A near Section 72. ca. May 2023

> An early settler was W. J. Sparkes, who died on January 7, 1851, aged 52. Beside him is his daughter, Rose Sparkes, who died in July, 1867, aged 16, and his mother-in-law, Elizabeth Bowes, who died on June 30, 1859, aged 70. An old resident who came here in 1853, says that Sparkes died two years before she arrived, that he kept a general store near St. Patrick's Tavern, and next Paddy Maine's shop, in Queen street.
